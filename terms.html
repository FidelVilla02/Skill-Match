Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 2026

1. What this platform is

SkillMatch Toledo is a student project built for an academic course. It is a demonstration of how a local employment platform could work. It is not a real recruitment service and no actual employment contract can be formed through it.

2. No diploma required

Anyone can use this platform to look for work or to hire. You do not need a college degree, a TESDA certificate, or any formal credential. How jobs are matched to people is based on skills and work experience only. If you choose to write down your education on your profile, that information is shown to employers as context and is never used to rank, filter, or exclude you.

3. Your account

You are responsible for keeping your login details to yourself, and for the accuracy of the information you enter. Anyone who can use your account is treated as acting on your behalf.

4. Your data

This is a frontend-only demonstration. Everything you type is stored in your own web browser using local storage. Nothing is transmitted to a server, and clearing your browser data will delete your accounts, job posts, and applications. Your contact number is shown to an employer only after that employer shortlists you.

5. Honest postings

Job posts must describe a role that genuinely exists. Employers must not use the platform to collect fees from applicants, and must not ask for payment, bank details, or identity documents in exchange for a job. Such posts may be removed.

6. Conduct

Do not post discriminatory listings, do not impersonate someone else, and do not send unwanted or repeated messages. Employers are responsible for treating all applicants fairly regardless of education, age, gender, or background.

7. No guarantee

The platform is provided as-is, with no warranty of accuracy, availability, or suitability. Match scores are a simple comparison of skills and experience, not a hiring decision.

8. Changes

These terms may be updated as the project develops. Continuing to use the platform after a change means you accept the revised terms.
