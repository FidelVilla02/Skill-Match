Find Work That Matches Your Skills

SkillMatch Toledo connects job seekers and employers across Toledo and neighbouring towns. Matching runs on the skills you already have and the experience you have picked up, whether that came from a diploma, a TESDA certificate, an apprenticeship, or learning on your own.

Get Started Browse Jobs

Open to everyone

You do not need a college diploma to use SkillMatch. TESDA holders, vocational graduates, skilled tradespeople, self-taught workers, career changers, and experienced professionals are all welcome here. Your employer chooses the requirements for their role, and you choose which of those roles to apply to.

How It Works

1

Create an account as a job seeker or an employer

2

Add your skills and experience, or post your opening

3

See a match score on every role, sorted for you

4

Apply, then follow your status through to a decision

The hiring pipeline is the same on both sides

  • AppliedThe employer reviews your application and your skills.
  • ShortlistedYou are interested in them, and your contact details are shared.
  • InterviewYou agree on a time and place, and see the employer's details.
  • HiredYou have the job.

You can withdraw at any time while you are still in Applied, Shortlisted, or Interview, and you can re-apply later. Salary is optional to post, so an honest employer can hire without hiding the pay behind a listing.
